Why it exists

If you pay per token, your invoice is a lagging indicator — you find out after the money is gone. agentledger answers the question while you are still working: what has this agent, this model, this week actually cost?

What it does

  • Reads local logs only — ~/.claude/projects, ~/.codex/sessions, ~/.gemini/tmp
  • Prices cache tokens correctly — cache reads and cache writes are billed at different rates from fresh input
  • Never touches the network — no account, no key, no telemetry, ~700 lines you can audit in one sitting
  • Fast — a full scan of 400M+ tokens takes about a second
